Platform guide

Getting started with Zyphor

Zyphor combines account-based messaging, profile customization, image storage, community events and other utilities. This guide describes what the visible website interfaces do and where to find them. Availability may differ by account, device and active event.

Open ZyphorContact support
Section 01

Discover the public website

Before signing in, use the homepage for an overview and the About page for background on how the platform is organized. The downloads portal introduces Android and Windows releases. For troubleshooting, this guide and the help center do not require an account.

Interactive areas are separate from public information pages. A dashboard is designed around the signed-in user and cannot replace public documentation for first-time visitors.

Section 02

Create and protect an account

Use the signup page to create an account and the login page to return. Review the service terms and privacy policy before submitting credentials. Some security workflows can require email verification or a separate second-factor screen. Never enter a password or one-time recovery code on a lookalike domain.

A successful login leads to the account area. If a previous session expires, sign in again through the official page. Never publish a session token or send it to another member, including someone claiming to be staff.

Section 03

Find friends and use messages

The messaging area combines a friends list and private conversations. Choose a friend from the list to open the conversation, then use the message composer. Availability of calls and group conversations can depend on user permissions and device support. A private message is not a public forum post; avoid sharing sensitive data even with trusted contacts.

On smaller screens, Zyphor provides an alternative mobile interface. The mobile and desktop layouts can differ in where they place the friend list, request controls and chat composer. If a feature seems missing, check the matching device layout first.

Section 04

Use your profile and settings

Profiles display account identity, badges and other information that may be visible to other members. Some elements are editable from account pages, while others reflect activity or permissions. Open Profile to access the current interface. Be mindful of what you publish: a profile photo or biography should not reveal personal or identifying information that you do not want others to see.

Membership and account settings may include feature-specific benefits. A membership does not grant permission to bypass community or platform rules.

Section 05

Store pictures with Zyphor Cloud

Zyphor Cloud is a photo-oriented area with controls for uploading, browsing, finding, favoriting, downloading and organizing images. The upload interface identifies PNG, JPG and JPEG formats. Confirm that a file upload has completed before leaving the page, especially over a mobile connection.

Only upload images that you own or have permission to store. Avoid using cloud storage as your only backup; keep an independent copy of important media.

Section 06

Explore events and collections

Seasonal event pages may display a global objective, collectible cases, rarity information and badges. The Halloween interface publishes rarity odds and shows recent openings; the collection page tracks obtained copies. Some experiences only operate during scheduled dates. Current event status comes from the server, so a static preview or outdated screenshot does not guarantee availability.

Trading proposes exchanges of collectibles or Zyphium. Check item identities, quantities and the other party before confirming. A trade that is completed server-side may not be reversible. Do not exchange real money outside official payment routes in response to a promise of rare items.

Section 07

Understand Zyphium and account balances

The wallet interface displays a Zyphium balance, recent activity and transfer controls. Zyphium is a platform-specific balance, not a statement of guaranteed cash value or redeemability. Transfer instructions require the correct recipient identifier; check the amount and identifier carefully before confirming.

Purchases, rewards, subscriptions and transfers should be reviewed through the relevant official interface. Do not send payment to a member who claims they can boost a balance or deliver an account upgrade outside the platform.

Section 08

Get help and report problems

Use the support center for login problems, billing questions, technical errors or suspected abuse. A helpful report includes the page address, the approximate time, what you expected and what happened instead. Screenshots may help, but remove passwords, private messages, session tokens and personal information first.

The community standards explain how to report harassment, fraud and other problematic activity. For app installation questions, consult the download and device guide.

Need assistance? Zyphor is updated frequently. If a button, feature or release is unavailable, use Support rather than relying on an outdated third-party download or tutorial. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
