Read before you sign in

Helpful, independent guides to the Zyphor platform

Zyphor is more than a collection of buttons. Learn how its account, communication, photo, collection and support systems work before you decide whether to use them. These guides remain publicly accessible.

Getting started

Follow a practical route through account creation, friends and messages, profile settings, Zyphor Cloud images, event participation, wallet balances and support. The guide explains which features require a session and what to check before transferring digital items.

Help and troubleshooting

Learn what to do when sign-in fails, the dashboard stays on a loading screen, a conversation is missing, an upload appears stuck or an Android notification does not arrive. Find a checklist of details that make support tickets easier to resolve without sharing secrets.

Community safety

Read the standards for public profiles, chats, reports, trade offers, user-uploaded media and public votes. Understand why staff impersonation, fake rewards, account theft, harassment and misleading trade claims are not acceptable.

App installation guide

Find advice for Android APK and Windows downloads, version checks, app permissions and security warnings. The guide explains why release files should come from the official downloads page, rather than links received from unknown accounts.

Advertising and privacy

See what Google advertising cookies may be used for, how personalized ads settings work, and why the EEA, UK and Switzerland require special consent safeguards. Account verification, ad consent and your account's login session are different things.

Project details

Discover how Zyphor's browser experience, account features and optional apps relate to one another, and where its project documentation lives. Check live feature status rather than interpreting a teaser or an upcoming release as a promise that an offer is already available.

About the project

A software platform that continues to evolve

Zyphor is an ongoing software project focused on building and maintaining digital experiences. Its features and applications develop over time as improvements, updates and new ideas are introduced.

This public page introduces the platform and provides a starting point for visitors who want to learn about its purpose before exploring its available features.
