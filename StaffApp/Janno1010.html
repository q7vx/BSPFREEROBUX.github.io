Main Areas To Improve

⚖️ Moderation Judgment

  • Don't treat every rule violation as an automatic punishment.
  • Consider context, intent, severity, evidence, and previous history.
  • Don't immediately jump to bans unless the situation actually warrants it.
  • Explain why a punishment is appropriate.

💬 Support

  • Don't tell frustrated users to "calm down."
  • Acknowledge their frustration.
  • Investigate the actual issue.
  • Don't imply that support will be withheld because someone is rude.

🛡️ Staff Conduct

  • Remain neutral when investigating reports.
  • Don't give friends special treatment.
  • Know when to escalate to higher staff.
  • Be careful with serious punishments such as bans and IP bans.

📝 Communication

  • Give more detailed explanations.
  • Use professional language when dealing with users.
  • Improve spelling and grammar.
  • Make responses clear enough that users understand the decision.
Overall: 5.5/10

Your strongest areas are fairness toward friends, knowing when to ask higher staff, acknowledging mistakes, and understanding the basic punishment system. Your weakest areas are contextual moderation, support handling, and explaining why a specific punishment is appropriate.
