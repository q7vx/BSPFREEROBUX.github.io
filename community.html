Trust & safety

Community standards and reporting

These guidelines set expectations for profiles, messages, public votes, uploaded content, collectible trading and other shared spaces. They complement, rather than replace, the Terms of Service.

Open ZyphorContact support
Section 01

Respect other people

Do not target other members with threats, harassment, hate, degrading remarks, repeated unwanted contact or non-consensual sharing of personal information. This applies to user profiles, usernames, public vote explanations, messages and submitted media. Do not impersonate staff or another member to gain trust or information.

Disagreement about a game, a trade or the platform is allowed. Persistent harassment, coordinated abuse and threats are not.

Section 02

Keep accounts and personal data safe

Do not ask another member for a password, session token, two-factor code, backup code or password reset link. Do not disclose somebody else's real name, contact information, location or other private data without appropriate permission. If you believe your account may be compromised, secure it through official account controls and contact Support.

Section 03

No scams or misleading reward claims

Never promise “free Robux,” guaranteed profit, special account upgrades or rare items in exchange for credentials, off-platform payments or installation of unknown software. If you discuss virtual items, represent quantities and drop rates honestly; do not forge proof of a transaction, pretend to act for Zyphor staff or misrepresent a third-party product as officially endorsed.

Public polls are community feedback and do not create an obligation to deliver a proposed feature or giveaway.

Section 04

No unauthorized software abuse

Do not use Zyphor to distribute malware, stolen credentials, pirated accounts or tools that grant unauthorized access to software or another person's device. Do not attempt to exploit Zyphor services, evade bans, flood APIs or manipulate game economies. Security issues should be reported privately through official support channels so they can be investigated safely.

Section 05

Responsible collections and trades

Confirm the exact items, unique item identifiers where shown, the recipient and the Zyphium amount before accepting a trade. Do not advertise fabricated odds or pretend a random reward is guaranteed. Do not use the trade system to deceive users or arrange an off-platform transfer that bypasses the safeguards presented in the interface.

Section 06

Uploads and public material

Only upload content that you have the right to use. Do not post sexual exploitation material, threats, malware, copyright-infringing reuploads or instructions for illegal behavior. Avoid putting identifying details into screenshots. Images and written material that are publicly displayed may be reported by other users.

Private cloud storage is not a license to upload or share illegal material. Local laws and service rules still apply.

Section 07

Reporting and moderation

When reporting a profile or item, choose the closest available category and provide objective details. Include a timestamp or screenshot when it helps, but redact private information. Moderators may review reports according to available tools and policies; reporting does not guarantee a particular outcome. Misusing reports to retaliate against somebody is not acceptable.

For urgent safety threats, contact the relevant local authorities rather than relying solely on a website ticket. For general issues, use Zyphor Support.

Need assistance? Zyphor is updated frequently. If a button, feature or release is unavailable, use Support rather than relying on an outdated third-party download or tutorial. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
