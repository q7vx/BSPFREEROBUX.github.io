Privacy & transparency

Advertising, cookies and privacy choices

Zyphor's account features, private conversations and stored images work differently from public pages. This explanation focuses on advertising-related tracking and the choices available when advertising is enabled. It is written to be read without signing in.

Full privacy policySupport
Section 01

Advertising status and scope

Zyphor uses Google AdSense as its intended publisher advertising provider. The site may retain an AdSense ownership-verification meta tag and an ads.txt authorization file even while ad loading is disabled. An authorization file is not itself an advertisement or a consent request. Only publicly accessible, substantive editorial pages should be considered for future ads; login, private chat, error and transaction screens are excluded.

Advertising activation depends on account approval, verified site eligibility, and any required consent-management setup. Neither an ad account nor this notice guarantees that Google ads will be displayed.

Section 02

Google cookies and third-party vendors

If AdSense ads are enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this website or to other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to Zyphor and other sites on the internet. Ads and related technologies may support delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and frequency limiting.

Additional third-party ad vendors may be used if enabled in the AdSense account; their identities and purposes must be included in the actual consent-management interface and publisher vendor disclosures. A list of vendors should be reviewed whenever advertising settings change.

Section 03

Personalization and opt-out options

Visitors can manage personalized Google advertising through My Ad Center and Google Ads Settings. Additional industry opt-out information is available at aboutads.info. Browser controls may also allow cookies to be deleted or limited.

Opting out of personalized advertising does not necessarily stop all advertising or all strictly necessary cookies. Where an authorized consent-management platform is active, use its controls to give or withdraw consent for the purposes it displays.

Section 04

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Google requires a Google-certified Consent Management Platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF) for relevant ad serving in these regions. Zyphor cannot replace that service with a homemade notice or a pre-checked checkbox. Choices must be passed to Google and its advertising partners according to the active rules. Consent must be obtained where legally required before accessing or storing nonessential information.

If a consent interface is not available yet, that does not authorize hidden tracking. Zyphor's AdSense display-ad loader stays disabled until consent and account configuration are verified by the site operator.

Section 05

Private account information versus ad data

Messages, authentication tokens, account passwords, private Cloud images and support conversations are not public article content and should not be inserted into advertisements. Do not share account credentials or session identifiers with an ad vendor or a stranger claiming to offer support. Site owners should avoid passing personally identifiable information to Google ad requests.

The broader categories of account data, service logs, storage and integrations are addressed in the Privacy Policy. Ad-related purposes should be described separately from the data strictly needed to operate account features.

Section 06

Questions, withdrawal and changes

When a certified consent dialog is deployed, visitors should be able to revisit choices through the mechanism offered by that dialog. If you cannot find a working control, contact Support rather than assuming a preference was saved. You can also change personalized ads settings with Google directly.

Zyphor's ad partners, purposes and technologies may change when advertising is activated or updated. This page must be kept consistent with the AdSense account settings and with the platform's full privacy policy.

These public explanations are not a substitute for the full Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, or any consent dialog displayed by an authorized consent-management platform. For assistance contact Support.
