Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about posting, participating, and keeping the Zyphor Community useful for everyone.

What is the Zyphor Community?

The forum is a place to discuss Zyphor, ask questions, share tutorials and discoveries, suggest features, and connect with other members.

How do I create a post?

Sign in to your Zyphor account, open the community forum, select Create a post, and enter a title and content. You can also choose a category, add up to six hashtags, and attach up to four images.

Can I reply to someone else's reply?

Yes. Open any discussion and click Reply on a comment. Replies can be nested inside replies, so conversations can continue at any depth. Use the replies button to expand a thread.

How do hashtags and search work?

Enter tags like #freerobux, #guide or #help when publishing. Click a hashtag to find other posts using it. The search bar finds posts by keywords in their titles, messages, and tags.

What are Trending, Top, Latest, and Unanswered?

Trending highlights recent activity and engagement. Top this week and Top all time emphasize posts with the highest vote scores. Latest is chronological, Recently active shows recently updated threads, and Unanswered lists discussions without published replies.

How do followers work?

Visit a member's profile and choose Follow. You will receive forum notifications when they publish a new post. Their profile also shows how many followers, posts, and replies they have.

Why can't I unfollow Somin?

Somin is the community founder and is included as a required follow for every account. This ensures everyone can receive founder announcements. Somin cannot be unfollowed through the forum.

What notifications can I receive?

New posts from followed people, replies to your discussions or replies, and administrator moderation notices. You can open the Notifications section and mark items as read.

Can I edit or delete my posts?

You can edit the title, content, category, and tags of your own posts. For removal or other moderation actions, contact a community administrator. Administrators may edit or remove content.

What happens when a post is placed under review?

It temporarily disappears from public listings until an administrator restores it or deletes it. Review actions are recorded so the moderation team can track decisions.

What image formats and file sizes are allowed?

You can add up to four images per post. Supported types are PNG, JPEG, WEBP, and GIF, with a limit of 5 MB per file. Don't upload private information, illegal content, or images you do not have permission to share.

What are the community guidelines?

Be respectful. Stay on topic. Do not spam, impersonate others, harass members, publish private information, or post harmful or illegal content. Do not distribute scams, phishing links, malware, or deceptive promotions. Administrators may review, edit, or remove violating posts and replies.

Can I save posts for later?

Yes. Use Save on a post and open Bookmarks from the sidebar. You can also upvote posts and replies that you find useful.

How do I report a problem?

Contact Zyphor support through the main website. Include a link to the discussion and a description of the issue. Avoid publicly sharing personal data in a report.
